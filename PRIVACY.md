# Privacy Policy — Privacy Curtain

_Last updated: November 17, 2026_

Privacy Curtain is a browser extension that blurs sensitive content on WhatsApp Web, Gmail, Microsoft Outlook and Microsoft Teams so people nearby or watching your screen share can't read it.

## Data we collect

**None.** Privacy Curtain does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal data or browsing data.

- No accounts, analytics, tracking, cookies or advertising.
- The extension makes no network requests.
- It does not read or record the content of your chats, emails or pages. It only adds CSS styling that blurs elements on the supported sites.

## Data stored on your device

Your blur settings (which items to blur on each site) are saved with the browser's built-in extension storage (`chrome.storage.sync`). If browser sync is turned on, your browser may sync these settings across your own devices. We never have access to them.

## Permissions

- **Storage:** saves your blur settings.
- **Site access:** limited to `web.whatsapp.com`, `mail.google.com`, `outlook.live.com`, `outlook.office.com`, `outlook.office365.com`, `outlook.cloud.microsoft`, `teams.microsoft.com`, `teams.live.com` and `teams.cloud.microsoft`. It is used only to apply the blur styling on those pages.
